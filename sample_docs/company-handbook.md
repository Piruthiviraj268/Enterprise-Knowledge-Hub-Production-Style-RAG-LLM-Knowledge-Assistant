# Northstar Labs — Company Handbook

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave should normally be requested at least five working days in advance through the HR portal. Managers may approve exceptions for urgent situations.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their role permits it. Customer-facing roles may have different requirements. Remote work must be recorded on the team calendar so colleagues know where coverage is available.

## Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 30,000 for approved courses, certifications, conferences and technical books. Purchases above INR 15,000 require manager approval before payment.
