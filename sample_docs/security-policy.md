# Northstar Labs — Security Policy

## Passwords
Corporate passwords must be unique and must not be reused on personal services. Passwords should be at least 14 characters and multi-factor authentication is required for production systems.

## Data classification
Internal information is intended for employees and approved contractors. Confidential information must only be stored in approved company systems. Public information can be shared externally after normal review.

## Incident reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within 30 minutes of discovery. Do not delete logs, rotate evidence unnecessarily, or contact an external party before the incident lead provides direction.
